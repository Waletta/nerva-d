Nerva brings the power of **500+ Large Language Models (LLMs)** and AI tools into a single, intuitive platform. Every day, we automatically integrate the newest state-of-the-art models as soon as they drop—ensuring you are always first to test cutting-edge AI breakthroughs.

There are **no software packages to install, no terminal commands to run, and no coding experience required**.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, all you need is:

- A modern web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox)
- An internet connection

## Get Started in 3 Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log In to the Portal">
    Navigate to the [Nerva Portal](https://nerva.live) and sign in with your email or preferred single sign-on (SSO) provider.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose from 500+ AI Models">
    Access our model library featuring **500+ LLMs** (including GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, and Qwen) alongside daily fresh additions, or select specialized tools for image, video, and voice generation.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start Creating">
    Type your prompt, compare model answers side-by-side, or upload your media files for instant, AI-powered processing in your browser.
  </Step>
</Steps>
