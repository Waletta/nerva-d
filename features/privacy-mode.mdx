---
title: "Privacy Mode (SNC Data)"
description: "Automatic sensitive data redaction for prompts and documents."
---

Pasting emails or documents into public AI models often leaks sensitive information like names, phone numbers, and addresses. **Privacy Mode** auto-hides sensitive parameters before transmitting prompts.

<Callout type="info">
Your sensitive data stays on your device. The AI processes anonymized tokens, ensuring compliance and peace of mind.
</Callout>

### How it Works
1. You paste text into Nerva.
2. Privacy Mode detects Personal Identifiable Information (PII).
3. PII is redacted locally before sending to external model endpoints.
